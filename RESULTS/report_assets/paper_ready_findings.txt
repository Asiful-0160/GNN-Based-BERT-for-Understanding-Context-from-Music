FINAL RESEARCH FINDINGS
=======================

Primary research question:
Does GNN+BERT fusion improve multi-label music-context understanding over either modality alone?

Overall TEST performance:
BERT mean AUC-PR = 0.83133318
Early Fusion mean AUC-PR = 0.83191649
Early Fusion - BERT AUC-PR = +0.00058331

BERT mean Macro-F1 = 0.78089422
Early Fusion mean Macro-F1 = 0.78198704
Early Fusion - BERT Macro-F1 = +0.00109281

BERT mean Micro-F1 = 0.78079060
Early Fusion mean Micro-F1 = 0.78421469
Early Fusion - BERT Micro-F1 = +0.00342410

Exploratory paired bootstrap:
AUC-PR 95% CI = [-0.00930631, +0.01039828]
Macro-F1 95% CI = [-0.00756224, +0.00916831]
Micro-F1 95% CI = [-0.00435494, +0.01088484]

Interpretation:
All exploratory confidence intervals include zero. Therefore the experiment does not provide evidence of a reliable overall performance advantage for Early Fusion over BERT.
Early Fusion and BERT should be described as essentially comparable overall, with a very small mean advantage for Early Fusion.

Audio-only comparison:
CNN mean AUC-PR = 0.22324414
GAT seed-42 AUC-PR = 0.18767503
Early Fusion clearly outperformed the audio-only baselines.

Largest label-specific effects:
Best AUC-PR gain: passionate (+0.046097)
Largest AUC-PR reduction: acoustic drums (-0.063072)

Reporting caution:
The bootstrap analysis is exploratory and post-hoc. Do not describe the tiny overall Fusion-BERT difference as statistically significant.
